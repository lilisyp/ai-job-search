# Behavioral Profile

## Overview
Li Li's self-assessment and manager reference (Roland Nunheim, June 2026) describe a **autonomous, evidence-led investor** pattern: comfortable building analysis from scratch in ambiguous deep-tech settings, presenting risks openly in IC, and influencing without formal authority as a board observer.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Analytical rigor | High | Data-first decisions; financial models and customer validation drive IC recommendations, not momentum |
| Autonomy & ownership | High | Thrives driving deals end-to-end in lean teams with direct access to decision-makers |
| Cross-functional influence | High | Bridges technology, capital, and operating stakeholders; board observer across four companies |
| Merit over politics | High | Will kill a deal on facts rather than push for optics; misaligned incentives are a drain |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Builds from scratch:** Constructs analysis and financial models when no playbook exists — deep-tech investing with no template is normal operating mode
- **Evidence-led communication:** Measured in IC rooms; presents case and risks openly without overselling; direct but respectful with founders on technical and commercial substance
- **Pattern recognition + depth:** ~20 years and hundreds of companies enable fast triage; investigates deliberately on what matters (e.g. isolating one technical variable before customer validation)
- **Cross-border bridge:** Bilingual (Mandarin native, English professional for memos and board materials); connects startups to corporate business units and international stakeholders

## How You Work Best
- High autonomy with high ambiguity; senior individual contributor driving deals sourcing → IC → close
- Lean teams, real ownership, direct access to decision-makers
- In-person for founder and board relationships; remote comfortable for analysis
- Cross-functional collaboration — strategy projects with top consultancies, BU connection work, board observer influence without formal authority

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Low tolerance for politics-over-merit environments:** Frame as disciplined capital allocation and fiduciary rigor — recommends against deals on valuation and management when facts don't support proceeding
- **Repetitive admin without judgment:** Frame as preference for analytical work where experience and judgment create value; delegate or systematize routine process

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "End-to-end deal ownership" / "lead investments from sourcing to close"
- "Comfortable with ambiguity" / "build the investment thesis"
- "Board observer" / "portfolio value creation" / "work with founders"
- "Cross-functional" / "bridge technical and commercial"
- "Evidence-based" / "IC-ready analysis" / "financial modelling"
- "Greater China" / "bilingual" / "Mandarin and English"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "Highly structured process" with no room for independent judgment
- "Sales-driven culture" / "close at all costs"
- Heavy admin or compliance-only roles with little analytical content
- Roles requiring relocation outside GBA
- Head / Director / C-suite ownership with heavy travel, frequent overnight trips, or founder-style hours (family constraint — two young children; prefer Manager / Senior Manager / AVP)

## Management Style Preferences
- Works well with leaders who give autonomy, value merit-based decisions, and engage directly on substance
- Struggles when internal politics override facts, or when repetitive admin displaces analytical judgment
